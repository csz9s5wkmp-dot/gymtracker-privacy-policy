# Privacy Policy for Gym Tracker

**Effective date:** November 1, 2026

Gym Tracker ("the app") is made by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## In short

We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. Everything you enter in the app stays on your device.

## 1. Information we collect

We collect no personal information. We have no servers, no user accounts and no sign-up.

The information you enter in the app is:

- the days you mark as gym visits
- your daily weight entries
- your goals (weekly gym goal and target weight), if you use Gym Tracker Pro

This information is saved only on your own device. We cannot see it, and it is never sent to us.

## 2. How your information is used

Your information is used only inside the app, to show you your calendar, streaks, weight chart and statistics. If you add the Gym Tracker widget to your home screen, a small summary (such as your streak and number of gym days) is shared between the app and the widget on your device so the widget can display it. This stays on your device.

## 3. Sharing and third parties

We do not share your information with anyone. The app contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no code from third parties that collects data. We do not sell your information.

The app does not connect to Apple Health and does not access your location, contacts, photos or camera.

## 4. In-app purchases

Gym Tracker Pro is available as an in-app purchase. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details, your Apple ID or your billing information. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

## 5. Exporting your data

Gym Tracker Pro lets you export your gym days and weights as a CSV file. This only happens when you choose it. The file goes where you send it (for example, to the Files app or another app of your choice). We do not receive it.

## 6. Backups and app analytics from Apple

Depending on your device settings, Apple may include the app's data in your iCloud or computer backups. This is controlled by you in your Apple settings and is not something we can access.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous, combined information such as crash reports or download counts. It cannot be used to identify you.

## 7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual weight entries inside the app and remove gym days by tapping them on the calendar. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## 8. Children's privacy

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. The latest version will always be available at the address where you are reading it now.
